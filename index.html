O−

Universal donor

O-negative blood can be given to patients of any blood type in an emergency — which is why it's always in short supply.

AB+

Universal recipient

Patients with AB-positive blood can safely receive donations from any blood type, making them the universal recipient.

Recovery window

Most donors can safely give whole blood again after about 8 weeks — your body fully replenishes red cells in that time.
